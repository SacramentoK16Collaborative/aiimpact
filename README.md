This interactive dashboard translates key findings from the AI in Allied Healthcare report into a dynamic, user-friendly tool for exploration and planning. Designed to support partners across the Sacramento K-16 Collaborative, it offers a real-time view of how artificial intelligence is reshaping healthcare roles and the skills required to succeed.

The dashboard is organized into five core sections: AI Impact Landscape, Role Transformation, Skill Explorer, Skill Category Transformation, and a Sacramento Overview. Together, these views provide insight into how specific roles are evolving, which skills are becoming more or less critical, and what these shifts mean for regional workforce development.

By making complex data accessible and actionable, the dashboard is intended to inform education and training design, strengthen alignment with employer needs, and support more responsive, equitable pathways into healthcare careers.
